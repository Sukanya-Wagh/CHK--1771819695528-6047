General Safety Tips
Before Use (वापरण्यापूर्वी)
  • Read label carefully
  • Wear protective equipment
  • Check weather conditions
  • Keep children and pets away
During Use (वापरताना)
  • Follow recommended dosage
  • Avoid spray drift
  • Don't eat, drink or smoke
  • Spray in morning or evening
After Use (वापरानंतर)
  • Wash hands and face thoroughly
  • Bathe with soap
  • Wash contaminated clothes separately
  • Store pesticide safely
Emergency (आपत्कालीन)
  • If swallowed: Seek medical help immediately
  • If on skin: Wash with soap and water
  • If in eyes: Rinse with water for 15 minutes
  • Call poison control: 1066
